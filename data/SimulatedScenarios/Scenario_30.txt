Scenario #28 
Patient Name: Mr. Liam Wallace
Age: 30 years
Background: Liam Wallace was known for his unmatched passion for marine biology. Working as a marine conservationist, he dedicated his life to studying and protecting coral reefs. His work often took him on diving expeditions, capturing breathtaking underwater photographs that he shared on his educational blog. Liam was also an advocate for sustainable fishing practices and conducted workshops in schools to educate children about marine ecosystems.
Living Situation: Living in a coastal town, Liam's house was a stone's throw away from the beach, filled with marine artifacts, research papers, and dive equipment. He was engaged to his childhood friend, Anna, who shared his love for the ocean and worked alongside him on several conservation projects.
Presenting Complaint: On one of his routine diving expeditions, Liam experienced difficulty in resurfacing and was later found unconscious by fellow divers. He was immediately rushed to a hospital with suspected decompression sickness, commonly known as "the bends."
Clinical Decision Point: On evaluation, Liam was found to have multiple air emboli in his bloodstream, affecting vital organs, including his brain. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy, a primary treatment for decompression sickness, was urgently recommended. However, given the delay in treatment and the severity of the emboli, there was a significant risk of permanent neurological impairment, even if the immediate crisis was managed.
Liam had previously discussed with Anna his fears about potential diving accidents, emphasizing that he wouldn't want any life-prolonging treatments if they resulted in a significant loss of cognitive or motor functions. However, now faced with the immediacy of the situation and the uncertainty of outcomes, Anna struggled with the decision.
While the medical team prepared for hyperbaric therapy, understanding its potential benefits, they were also cognizant of Liam's previously expressed wishes. Anna, torn between honoring Liam's stated preferences and her hope for his recovery, faced an incredibly challenging decision.
