Scenario #8
Patient Name: Ms. Julia Roberts
Age: 50 years
Background: Ms. Julia Roberts is a 50-year-old graphic designer with a passion for arts and crafts. She was actively involved in community events and workshops and loved spending weekends at local art galleries. Julia has been living with her partner, Samantha, for the past 20 years. They were never legally married, but they built a life together, sharing a close-knit circle of friends. Julia's immediate family includes her parents and a younger brother.
Current Living Situation: Julia and Samantha live in a cozy apartment filled with Julia's artwork. Both Samantha and Julia's family have been present at the hospital, but their differing views on Julia's care have created tension.
Presenting Complaint: Julia suffered a sudden and severe stroke, which left her neurologically devastated. She's currently in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and has been unable to regain significant brain function.
Clinical Findings: Julia is currently on mechanical ventilation due to her inability to breathe on her own. She is also unable to swallow or take in nutrition orally.
Clinical Decision Point: Given the prolonged need for ventilation and the challenges with feeding, the medical team is considering a tracheostomy to facilitate long-term ventilation and a percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tube for nutritional support. However, Samantha believes that Julia wouldn't want to live with such interventions, given the quality of life they would offer. She is advocating for withdrawal of care, believing it aligns more closely with Julia's values and wishes. In contrast, Julia's immediate family, particularly her parents, want all life-sustaining treatments to continue, holding onto hope for any potential improvement.
