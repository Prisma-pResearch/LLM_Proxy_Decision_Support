Scenario #37
Patient Name: Mr. Diego Rodriguez
Age: 40 years
Background: Mr. Diego Rodriguez, a construction site manager, recently moved to the city for a new project.  Diego, originally from another country, had limited connections in the city, and his closest family members were back in his home country.
Current Living Situation: He had been living in temporary housing while he looked for a permanent residence.
Presenting Complaint: Diego was involved in a severe car collision while commuting to work. Bystanders called emergency services, and he was promptly extricated from the wreckage and transported to the nearest trauma center. On arrival, he was unconscious with multiple fractures and suspected internal injuries.
Clinical Situation: After immediate resuscitation efforts, Diego was taken to the operating room for emergency surgery to address his life-threatening injuries. Postoperatively, he was admitted to the ICU on mechanical ventilation due to traumatic brain injury. Imaging revealed a diffuse axonal injury, indicating a significant brain injury with an uncertain prognosis.
The hospital staff initiated efforts to contact Diego's next of kin based on the limited information available in his wallet and the emergency contact details he provided to his employer. The listed contact was his sister, but the international phone number provided was not functional. Repeated attempts to contact other potential family members or close friends were unsuccessful.
