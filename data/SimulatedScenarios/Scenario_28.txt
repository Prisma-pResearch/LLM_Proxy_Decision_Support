Scenario #26
Patient Name: Mrs. Evelyn Brooks
Age: 83 years
Background: Mrs. Evelyn Brooks, once a renowned pianist, spent her golden years surrounded by music, often reminiscing about her performances around the world. Her graceful fingers, which once danced on piano keys, now mostly rested due to advanced rheumatoid arthritis. However, her spirit was untouched by her physical limitations. She cherished her time spent with her family, especially her great-granddaughter, Lucy, who showed a budding interest in music.
Living Situation: Evelyn lived in an old Victorian house, filled with mementos of her illustrious career. She had a dedicated caregiver due to her limited mobility but remained mentally sharp, often engaging in lively debates about classical music's evolution.
Presenting Complaint: During one of her afternoon piano lessons with Lucy, Evelyn suddenly complained of severe breathlessness and chest pain. An ambulance was promptly called, and she was rushed to the nearest hospital. Preliminary investigations revealed a massive pulmonary embolism (PE) — a sudden blockage in one of the pulmonary arteries in her lung.
Clinical Decision Point: The medical team quickly assessed that a procedure to remove or dissolve the clot could potentially save Evelyn's life. However, given her age and multiple comorbidities, including her heart condition and recent history of gastrointestinal bleeding, the procedure carried significant risks. There was a substantial possibility she could suffer from major complications, including severe bleeding, during or after the procedure.
The medical team, understanding the gravity of Evelyn's situation, was concerned about the procedure's potential benefits against its risks. Yet, Evelyn's family, having seen her indomitable spirit and her joy in life, especially in her bond with young Lucy, were adamant. They wanted the medical team to do everything in their power to save her, valuing every potential moment with their beloved matriarch.
