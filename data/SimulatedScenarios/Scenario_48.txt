Scenario #46
Patient Name: Mrs. Naomi Lewis
Age: 65 years
Background: Mrs. Naomi Lewis is a retired schoolteacher, known for her dedication to her students and her active involvement in community service. A devout member of a tight-knit religious community, she holds strong beliefs that heavily influence her decisions, including medical ones.
Living Situation: Unknown.
Presenting Complaint: Naomi began experiencing severe chest pains while at a community gathering. Recognizing the symptoms, a fellow attendee called for an ambulance, and she was rushed to the emergency department. The initial assessment indicated that Naomi had suffered a myocardial infarction (heart attack).
Clinical Situation: The cardiology team identified a significant blockage in one of Naomi's coronary arteries. The recommended treatment is an emergent coronary angioplasty with stent placement. The procedure, while routine and having a high success rate, does carry some risks, especially without the use of certain blood products due to potential bleeding complications.
Upon reviewing Naomi's medical chart, the team noticed a note indicating her refusal of certain blood-derived products based on her religious beliefs. The specific products the cardiologists would typically use during angioplasty to prevent clotting complications are among those she has refused.
Family Input: Naomi's daughter, Rachel, arrives at the hospital and confirms her mother's religious stance. She emphasizes that while they want the best care for Naomi, it has to be within the boundaries of their faith. However, Naomi's longtime friend, Elaine, pulls a doctor aside and shares that Naomi had confided in her about feeling conflicted; while she deeply respected her community's beliefs, she also expressed a wish to live long enough to see her grandchildren grow up, even if it meant bending some religious rules.
