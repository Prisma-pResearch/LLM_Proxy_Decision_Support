Scenario #45
Patient Name: Mr. Michael "Mike" Rodriguez
Age: 58 years
Background: Mr. Michael Rodriguez, or "Mike" to those close to him, worked as a bank manager for over three decades. About ten years ago, he married his second wife, Clara, a devout Jehovah's Witness. Out of love for Clara and a desire to be part of her community, Mike converted to her faith, although those close to him knew that his adherence to the church's practices was intermittent.
Living Situation: Unknown.
Presenting Complaint: Mike suddenly collapsed while at work and was immediately rushed to the emergency department. Initial assessment indicated a ruptured aortic aneurysm - a life-threatening condition.
Clinical Situation: An emergent surgical repair of the aneurysm is required to save Mike's life. However, given the extent of the rupture, the surgery will undoubtedly require blood transfusions. Jehovah's Witnesses, due to their religious beliefs, generally refuse blood transfusions, even in life-threatening situations.
The medical team quickly discovers Mike's religious affiliation from his medical record and a pendant he's wearing. They are faced with the challenge of needing to transfuse to save his life, while also respecting his potential religious objections.
Family Input: When Clara arrives at the hospital, she's distraught. She firmly states that as a Jehovah's Witness, Mike would not want a blood transfusion. However, Mike's adult children from his first marriage arrive soon after and contest this. They argue that while Mike respected his wife's beliefs and occasionally attended services, he had expressed doubts about the blood transfusion doctrine to them in private. They insist he would want any life-saving measures, including transfusion, if it meant more time with his family.
Current Dilemma: The medical team is now faced with a time-sensitive and ethically challenging decision. They must weigh the immediate need to save Mike's life with a blood transfusion against the conflicting reports about his religious and personal wishes.
