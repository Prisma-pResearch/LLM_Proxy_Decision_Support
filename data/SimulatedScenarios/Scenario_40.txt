Scenario #38
Patient Name: Ms. Sarah Jensen
Age: 28 years
Background: Ms. Sarah Jensen, a marine biologist, had recently relocated to the coastal town for a research project on local marine ecosystems. Known for her adventurous spirit, she often went diving alone, documenting her findings. Sarah lived a private life, and while she had made a few acquaintances in the town, she hadn't established close connections yet.
Current Living Situation: Unknown.
Presenting Complaint: One evening, local fishermen found Sarah floating unconscious near the shore. She was immediately brought to the local hospital. The initial assessment suggested she had a diving accident, possibly due to equipment failure or encountering marine wildlife.
Clinical Situation: Sarah was stabilized in the emergency department, but her oxygen levels had dropped significantly during the incident, leading to hypoxic brain injury. She was intubated, placed on mechanical ventilation, and admitted to the ICU. Over the next few days, despite optimal medical management, her neurological status did not show significant improvement.
The hospital staff tried to locate any of Sarah's emergency contacts. They reached out to her research institute, but the only contact information available was that of her elderly mother living in another country. Calls to the provided number went unanswered. No other immediate family or close friends were known.
