Scenario #2
Patient Name: Mrs. Lydia Bennett
Age: 43 years
Background: Mrs. Lydia Bennett is a 43-year-old accountant who enjoys an active lifestyle, including marathons, hiking with her family, and rock climbing. She has been married for 20 years to Mr. Thomas Bennett, a high school science teacher. They have two children: a teenage son, Jacob (16 years old), and a daughter, Emma (12 years old). Apart from occasional seasonal allergies, Lydia has always been in good health.
Current Living Situation: The Bennett family lives in a two-story home with a large backyard where Lydia maintained a garden. Lydia had been the primary caregiver for the children, managing their daily activities, schoolwork, and extracurriculars. Thomas is supportive and shares household responsibilities, but his job keeps him busy during weekdays.
Presenting Complaint: Over the past few months, Lydia noticed a growing lump on her upper thigh, close to her hip. Initially dismissing it as a possible muscle strain or benign growth, she grew concerned when the lump increased in size and became painful. During a weekend visit to a local rock climbing gym, Lydia experienced a fall from a significant height. Even though she was using safety equipment, the angle and force of the fall caused her to hit her head hard against the climbing wall. She was immediately rendered unconscious. On-site medical personnel provided initial care, and she was quickly transported to the hospital. Upon evaluation, she was diagnosed with a traumatic brain injury, leaving her incapacitated and unable to communicate or make decisions for herself.
Investigations: A biopsy of the lump was performed, revealing a high-grade soft tissue sarcoma. An MRI of the leg and pelvis confirmed the aggressive nature of the tumor, which had invaded nearby structures.
Clinical Decision Point: The oncology and surgical team convened to discuss Lydia's situation. The presence of the high-grade soft tissue sarcoma necessitates aggressive intervention. The best chance for a cure would be a hemipelvectomy, a radical surgery that involves amputation of the leg at the hip, along with a portion of the pelvis. However, Lydia's traumatic brain injury complicates the decision-making and potential recovery process.
