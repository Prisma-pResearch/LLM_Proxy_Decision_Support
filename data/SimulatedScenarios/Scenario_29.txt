Scenario #27
Patient Name: Ms. Sophie Martinez
Age: 27 years
Background: Sophie Martinez was the beating heart of her community. Her infectious enthusiasm for life was evident in her commitment to rescuing and rehabilitating stray cats through her local pet adoption center. Between juggling the demands of her center and planning her upcoming wedding, Sophie found solace in hiking and documenting her adventures on a travel blog.
Living Situation: Her cozy apartment, adorned with souvenirs from her travels and the comforting purrs of her rescued cats, was a testament to her vibrant life. Sophie was eagerly anticipating her wedding to her college sweetheart, with plans for a honeymoon backpacking across Europe.
Presenting Complaint: A weekend hiking trip took a tragic turn when Sophie slipped, resulting in a severe spinal injury. Airlifted to the nearest trauma center, the diagnosis was grim: a complete spinal cord injury at the T6 level, leading to paralysis from the mid-chest downwards.
The weight of the diagnosis, combined with her previously active lifestyle, plunged Sophie into acute distress. Inpatient therapy and counseling were initiated to help her process her new reality, but Sophie's despair persisted. She often expressed feelings of hopelessness, questioning the worth of a life so drastically different from the one she'd envisioned.
Clinical Complication: During her hospital stay, Sophie developed deep vein thrombosis (DVT) in her lower extremities, a common complication from prolonged immobility. The medical team recommended placing an IVC filter to prevent a life-threatening pulmonary embolism. However, Sophie, in her current emotional state, refused the intervention, expressing a desire to avoid any further medical treatments. She felt trapped in a body she no longer recognized and was resistant to interventions that would prolong her perceived suffering.
Her family, especially her fiancé, grappled with her refusal. They understood her profound grief but firmly believed that, given time and support, Sophie could adapt and find joy in life again. The urgency of the medical decision, juxtaposed with Sophie's current mental state, posed a significant dilemma for her care team.
