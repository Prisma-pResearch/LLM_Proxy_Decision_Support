Scenario #29
Patient Name: Ms. Isabella Torres
Age: 35 years
Background: Isabella Torres, an accomplished architect, had a flair for blending modern designs with sustainable elements. Her work was featured in various architectural magazines, and she was known for her dedication to creating eco-friendly urban spaces. Aside from her professional achievements, Isabella was an active community volunteer, often leading urban gardening projects and neighborhood clean-ups. 
Living Situation: She lived in a penthouse of a building she designed, with a rooftop garden that was her personal haven.
Presenting Complaint: While attending a construction site for a new project, an unexpected structural collapse occurred. Isabella was trapped under heavy debris for several hours before rescue teams could safely extract her. She was immediately transported to the trauma center of a nearby hospital with multiple injuries.
Clinical Findings: On evaluation, Isabella was found to have a severe traumatic brain injury (TBI), multiple rib fractures, a punctured lung, and a crushed pelvis. She was intubated due to respiratory distress and required emergency surgery to stabilize her pelvic injuries.
Clinical Decision Point: Post-operatively, while her physical injuries were addressed, Isabella remained in a comatose state due to her TBI. Neurological assessments painted a grim picture; there was a significant possibility of long-term cognitive impairment or a persistent vegetative state.
Isabella had previously shared with her sister, Maria, her thoughts on life-sustaining treatments following a documentary they watched together on TBI patients. She had expressed a strong wish against prolonged life support or interventions if she were ever to be in a state where meaningful recovery was unlikely.
Now, with Isabella's condition uncertain and the potential for some recovery, albeit with significant impairments, Maria faced a heart-wrenching decision. The trauma team, equipped with the medical facts, looked to Maria to guide them on the next steps for Isabella's care, balancing potential medical interventions against Isabella's previously expressed desires.
