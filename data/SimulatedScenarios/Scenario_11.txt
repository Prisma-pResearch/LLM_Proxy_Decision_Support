Scenario #9
Patient Name: Mr. Lucas Bennett
Age: 24 years
Background: Mr. Lucas Bennett is a 24-year-old individual with a known history of severe schizophrenia. Lucas was diagnosed in his late teens and has had multiple hospitalizations due to his psychiatric condition. Despite medical management, his schizophrenia remains only partially controlled, with periods of lucidity interspersed with acute episodes. Before his diagnosis, Lucas was an aspiring musician and had a close circle of friends.
Current Living Situation: Lucas resides in a group home that specializes in the care of individuals with severe psychiatric conditions. The staff are well-acquainted with his medical and psychiatric history. His parents are involved in his life, visiting weekly and participating actively in both his psychiatric and medical care decisions.
Presenting Complaint: Lucas had previously undergone surgery for a complicated intestinal issue, resulting in the creation of an ileostomy. Now, with the underlying issue resolved, he is a candidate for an elective ileostomy takedown.
Clinical Findings: The site of the ileostomy is healthy, and previous imaging studies indicate that the takedown procedure has a good chance of success. However, Lucas's psychiatric condition poses a challenge. While he expresses a desire to have the ileostomy reversed during his moments of lucidity, his consent and understanding of the procedure can fluctuate with his mental state.
Clinical Decision Point: The medical team recognizes the potential benefits of the ileostomy takedown for Lucas's physical health and quality of life. However, ensuring informed consent and post-operative compliance, given his severe schizophrenia, is a significant concern.
